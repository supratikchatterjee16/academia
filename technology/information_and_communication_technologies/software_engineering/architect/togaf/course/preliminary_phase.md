# Preliminary Phase

This phase creates the architecture capability of an architecture.

- Preparation and initiation activities to create an Architecture Capability
    - Defining roles and responsibilities
    - Form Architecture Teams, and Partition
    - Define the processes
    - Define Guidelines
    - Introduce tools and methods
- Customizing the TOGAF framework
- Definition of Architecture Principles


Objectives:
1. Determine the Architecture Capability desired by the organization
    - Review the organizational context for conducting EA
    - Identify and scope the elements of the enterprise organizations affected by the Architecture Capability
    - Identify the established frameworks, methds, and processes that intersect with the Architecture Capability
    - Establish the Capability Maturity target
2. Establish the Architecture Capabiltiy
    - Define and establish the Organizational Model for EA
    - Define and establish the detailed process and resources for Architecture Givernance
    - Select and implement tools that support the Architecture Capability
    - Define the Architecture Principles

## Steps

1. Scope the enterprise organizations impacted
2. Confirm governance and support frameworks
3. Define and establish EA teams and organizations
4. Identify and establish Architecture Principles
5. Tailor the TOGAF framework and, if any other selected architecture frameworks
6. Develop a strategy and implementation plan for tools and techniques

## Architecture Principles

They are made up of:

- Business Principles
- Data Principles
- Application Principles
- Technology Principles


- These are principles that guide architecture development and should be maintained in the Principles Catalog
- Define the underlying general rules & guidelines for the use of resources and assets
- Form the basis for making architecture decisions
- These are intended to be enduring and seldom ammended
- Reflect a level of consensus among the various elements of the enterprise
- Relate back to the business objectives and key architecture drivers
- Influenced by emerging industry trends, enterprise mission & plans, enterprise strategic initiatives, current systems & technology and external constraints
- Provide a foundation for making architecture and planning decisions, framing policies, procedures and standards and supporting resolution of conflicting situations
- Typically developed by the Enterprise Architects in conjunction with key stakeholders, being approved by the Architecture Board afterwards
- Altered and removed through an amendment process after initial ratification
- Few in number, future-oriented and endorsed & championed by senior management

The principles are structured hierarchically:

- Top most: Enterprise Principles
- Middle: Segment principles
- Bottom: Architecture Principle, and other principles

At each hierarchy level, the principles will be elaborate on the principles inherited from the level above, and the lower-level principle cannot overstep the boundaries of a higher-level principle.

TOGAF standard differentiates between Enterprise Principles ad Architecture Principles.

Enterprise Principles:
- Provide a basis for decision making throughout an enterprise
- Inform and support how an organization sets about fulfilling it's mission
- Are a key element in a successful Architecture Governance strategy
- Can have subsidiary principles within a business or organizational unit

Architecture Principles:
- Relate to Architecture Work
- Govern the architecture process
- Embody the spirit of thinking of existing Enterprise Principles

Structure of an architecture principle:

- Name: Represent the essence of the priciple and is easy to remember
- Statement: Communicates succicintly and unambiguosly the fundamental rule
- Rationale
    - Highlights the business benefits of adhering to the principle in business terms
    - Describes relationship to other principles, esp. where tradeoffs will be required
    - Gives guidance towards a balanced and consistent interpretation
- Implications
    - Highlight the requirements for carrying out the principle in terms of resources, costs and activities
    - State the impact to business and consequences of adopting the principle
    - Answer the "How does this affect me?" question of the reader

Example:

- Name: Compliance with Law
- Statement: Enterprise information management processes comply with all relevant laws, policies and regulations
- Rationale:
    - Enterprise policy is to abide by laws, policies and regulations
    - This will not preclude business process improvements that lead to changes in policies and regulations
- Implications:
    - Comply with laws, regulations and external policies regarding the collection, retention and management of data
    - Education and access to the rules
    - Changes in the law and changes in the regulation may drive changes in the business processes or applications

**5 criterias to distinguish a good set of principles**:
- Complete: Each principle is defined and every situation perceived is covered
- Consitent: Expressed in a way that allows a balance of flexible interpretations without contradictions
- Stable: Principles should be enduring, yet able to accommodate changes
- Understandable: Intentions of the principles are clear, understandable & unambiguous so that(unintentended) vialoations are minimized
- Robust: Enable good quality and consistent decisions about architectures, plans enforceable policies as well as standards to be created in complex situations

Purposes for the architectural principles:
- Enable decision-making: Set precedence during trade-off discussions and authority of tie-braking
- Align the enterprise: Take subjectivity and bias out and drive critical conversations aligned with the enterprise's value
- Support governance: Ensure that the right decisions are made at the right time with the right decision-makrs and that these decisions are monitored
- Reflect Value and Culture: Provide understanding about the enterprise's culture and values as well as an insight into how well the enterprise reacts to change
