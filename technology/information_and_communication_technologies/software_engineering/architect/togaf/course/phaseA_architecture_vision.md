# Phase A: Architecture Vision

This is the phase that creates the Archtiecture Vision and establishes the Architecture Project.

- Initial phase of the Architecture Development Cycle
- Defining the scope(problem and project)
- Identify stakeholders & concerns
- Create an Architecture Vision
- Obtain approval to proceed with the Architecture Development according to the Architecture Vision

## Objectives

- Develop a high-level aspirational vision of the capabilities and business value to be delivered as a result of the proposed Enterprise Architecture
- Obtain approval for Statement of Architecture Work that defines a program of works to develop and deploy the architecture outlined in the Architecture Vision

## Steps

1. Establish the architecture project
    - Need to be familiar with the project management process and approvals to go through to be confirmed as a project inside an organisation
    - Confirm the ability of the team to deliver a project
2. Identify stakeholders, concerns and business requirements
    - Involve the stakeholders and prepare the **stakeholder map** that shows which members are involved, level of involvement, and key concerns
    - **stakeholder map** is extremely important for the vision
3. Confirm and elaborate business goals, business drivers and constraints
    - May already be defined, and should be clarified during the formulation of the vision
    - Define the constraints, such as Enterprise(principles from the initial phase) and Project(time & budget) specific constraints
4. Evaluate Capabilities
    - Evaluate if the current capabilities are sufficient to carry out the architectural capability
    - Full capability assessment is part of Phase B: Business Architecture
5. Assess readiness for business transformation
    - Evaluate the organisational readiness to undergo change
    - Larger scope, means larger change, and larger resistance
    - Identify risks
6. Define scope
    - What is inside? What is beyond the baseline and target architecture to develop?
    - First - define the breadth of the architecture this project is concerned with.
    - Second - define depth of architecture. How much architecture is required to address the problem at hand?
    - Third - define the architecture domains that you want to cover with the project, based on the 4 domains, Business, Data, Application, or Technology
    - Fourth - define the time period
7. Confirm and elaborate Architecture Principles including business principles
    - Good starting point is to use business scenarios to discover and document business requirements
8. Develop Architecture Vision
    - Draw a simple solution concept diagram to roughly map the target architecture
9. Define the Target Architecture value propositions and KPIs
    - Develop the business case for the arch, and change required
    - Produce value propositions for each stakeholder group
    - Assess and define the procurement requirements
    - Review and agree on the value propositions with sponsors and stakeholder
    - Define the performance metrics, and measures to be built into the architecture.
        - Develop the business case for your architecture and changes required
        - Formulate value proposition for the architecture vision for each stakeholder group
10. Identify the business transformation risks and mitigation activities
11. Develop statement of Architecture work; secure approval
    - Key deliverable documenting key findings and results of this phase
    - It is a contract between the architecture team and the sponsors

## Request for Architecture Work - Establishes the architecture project

It is an order from the sponsoring organization to the architecture team.

Purpsose:
- High level document that is sent from the sponsoring to the architecture organization triggering the start of an architecture development cycle
- Created as output of the Preliminary Phase, results of an approved architecture Change Request or is generated as terms of reference for architecture work originating from migration planning

Contains:
- Organization sponsors, mission statements, budget information, constraints
- Business goals, strategic plans, changes in environment, time limits, constraints
- Business system, IT system, and architecture description
- Developing organization including resources

## Communications Plan from Stakeholder Map

Purpose:
- Allows communication to be carried out within a planned and managed process
- Ensures effective communication of targeted information to the right stakeholders at the right time as critical success factor for Enterprise Architecture

Contains:
- Stakeholders(Eg: grouping of communication requirements)
- Information(Eg: Needs, key messages for the vision, risks, success factors)
- Mechanisms(Eg: Meetings, newsletters, repositories)
- Timetable(Eg: Stakeholder <> time <> location)
